## 👋 Hello from the FixMate team

We're building **FixMate**, a full-stack web application that brings home-service discovery and coordination into a single, practical experience. From plumbing and electrical maintenance to cleaning, air-conditioning service, moving, and garden care, our goal is to make finding and working with service professionals more straightforward.

**FixMate is an actively developed Full-Stack Web Development course project.** This organization is the shared home for its code, documentation, and collaboration—not a claim that the service is commercially launched.

## 🧩 What we're building

| For customers | For service providers | For administrators |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Find services and local professionals | Create and manage a provider profile | Review provider applications |
| Search by service and governorate | Configure offerings, service areas, and schedules | Manage service catalog and account status |
| Request appointments or describe projects | Respond to bookings and project requests | Monitor and moderate platform activity |
| Follow progress and review completed work | Track work and availability | Support trustworthy marketplace operations |

### Designed around real everyday needs

- **Service discovery:** Structured categories, search, local filtering, and indicative pricing in Jordanian dinars.
- **Bookings and requests:** Appointment workflows and provider-directed project requests.
- **Bilingual experience:** Arabic-first RTL and English LTR interfaces, responsive from mobile to desktop.
- **Account roles:** Dedicated customer, provider, and administrator experiences.
- **Safety-minded engineering:** Protected APIs, role-based permissions, and session management.

## ✨ Our approach

We focus on **clear interfaces, maintainable architecture, and real functionality** over flashy claims. The project is developed iteratively, with responsive design, localization, automated checks, and documented decisions treated as part of the product—not afterthoughts.

We also distinguish **implemented functionality** from future ideas. Online payments, production messaging integrations, and public deployment are not represented here as completed capabilities.
